# Medical Quiz

## Question 1
According to the criteria for primary amenorrhea, at what age should a clinical evaluation begin if a patient lacks secondary sexual characteristics and has not yet experienced menarche?

- [x] 14 years
- [ ] 15 years
- [ ] 12 years
- [ ] 16 years

**Hint:** The age threshold is slightly lower when secondary sexual development is also absent.

## Question 2

- [x] Previous stroke or TIA
- [ ] Diabetes mellitus

**Hint:** Consider which factors represent the highest direct recurrence risk based on previous neurological events.

## Question 3
What is the primary recommendation for the detection of coronary artery disease in asymptomatic individuals?

- [x] Systematic screening is not recommended
- [ ] Annual electrocardiograms for all adults
- [ ] Stress testing for patients over 50
- [ ] Routine carotid ultrasound

**Hint:** The guidelines emphasize the management of risk factors over diagnostic testing for those without symptoms.

## Question 4

- [x] Secretory diarrhea
- [ ] Osmotic diarrhea
- [ ] Steatorrhea
- [ ] Inflammatory diarrhea

**Hint:** Think about whether the diarrhea is caused by unabsorbed solutes or active ion transport.

## Question 5

- [x] $Pneumocystis \ jirovecii$
- [ ] $Streptococcus \ pneumoniae$
- [ ] $Staphylococcus \ aureus$
- [ ] $Legionella \ pneumophila$

**Hint:** This organism was traditionally classified as $P. \ carinii$ and typically presents with an interstitial pattern on X-ray.

## Question 6
Which diagnostic tool is considered a good screening test for detecting cardiomyopathy associated with Chagas disease?

- [x] 12-lead ECG with a 30-second strip
- [ ] Chest X-ray
- [ ] Barium swallow
- [ ] Serial troponin levels

**Hint:** The most common findings for this condition include right bundle branch block and left anterior hemiblock.

## Question 8
In the context of Systemic Sclerosis (Scleroderma), what is the clinical significance of the 'watermelon stomach' (GAVE)?

- [x] It is a cause of recurrent GI bleeding and chronic anemia
- [ ] It indicates a high risk for gastric adenocarcinoma
- [ ] It is the primary cause of severe Gastroesophageal Reflux (GERD)
- [ ] It represents a benign finding with no clinical impact

## Question 9
Which of the following is considered an organic cause of hypercoagulability leading to potential neurological complications?

- [x] Antiphospholipid Syndrome (APS)
- [ ] Hyperthyroidism
- [ ] Vitamin $B_{12}$ deficiency
- [ ] Hypoxia

**Hint:** Look for an autoimmune condition often associated with both arterial and venous thrombosis.

## Question 10

- [x] Allow a 12-hour interval between the two medications
- [ ] Discontinue Clopidogrel immediately
- [ ] Double the dose of the PPI
- [ ] Take both medications simultaneously with food

**Hint:** The recommendation focuses on minimizing the pharmacological competition between the drugs.

## Question 11
Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) is characterized by the degeneration of which specific structures?

- [x] Both Upper and Lower Motor Neurons
- [ ] Exclusively Upper Motor Neurons
- [ ] The myelin sheath of the Peripheral Nervous System only
- [ ] The Basal Ganglia

**Hint:** Think about the 'Amiotrófica' part of the name, which implies muscle wasting from the loss of nerve signals.

## Question 12
In the diagnosis of protein-losing enteropathy, which clinical finding would suggest a renal or hepatic origin instead of an intestinal one?

- [x] Reduced albumin with normal globulin
- [ ] Simultaneous reduction of albumin and globulin
- [ ] Presence of peripheral edema
- [ ] Normal VES and CRP levels

**Hint:** Differentiating these depends on whether both large and small proteins are being lost indiscriminately.
